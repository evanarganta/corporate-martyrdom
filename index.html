COMMAND GUIDE

Controls

  • Left Click Drag / WASD: Pan Viewport
  • Mouse Scroll: Zoom In / Out
  • 1 - 9: Select Item in Category (Press again to cancel)
  • Z X C V B: Switch Category
  • Q: Demolish Mode / Cancel Placement
  • E: Confirm Action
  • R: Rotate Placement
  • T: Research Matrix
  • P: Production Analytics
  • Space: Pause / Resume
  • Esc: Close Windows

Logistics

  • Place drills directly over ore deposits.
  • Drills eject materials from their output face onto conveyors.
  • Smelters process ores into pure metal bars.
  • Fabricators assemble components and circuit boards.
  • Power poles connect machines to generators.
  • Conveyors connect seamlessly into corners.
